Arrival by air

Land at
the gate.

A helipad inside the paddock and a 750 m strip alongside the circuit. Guests land and walk to reception - no transfer, no convoy, no hour on the M7.

The strip takes light aircraft and turboprops. For anything larger, Budapest airport is an hour away by road and Vienna two and a quarter.
